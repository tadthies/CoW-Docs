# Menu Bar

The menu bar at the top of every page holds the display mode toggle, the help menu and your account menu.

## Display Mode

Click the sun or moon icon to switch between light mode and dark mode. On a narrow screen, the toggle is in the account menu instead, as **Light Mode** or **Dark Mode**.

## Help & Support

The help menu lists the ways to get help with COW.

### Discord

Opens the CyberDrain Discord server, which has channels for questions about COW.

### Docs

Opens this documentation at docs.cow.tech.

### Email Support

Opens a new email to COW support in your email application, with your organisation ID in the subject line. Replace the placeholder in the subject before sending. **Email Support** is not shown on the free tier.

## Account

The account menu has **Log out**. On a narrow screen it also shows the signed-in user and organisation domain, and the display mode toggle.
